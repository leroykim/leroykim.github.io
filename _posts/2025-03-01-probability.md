## Basics
### Conditional Probability
$$P(A|B)=\frac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)}$$
$P(A)$: the prior
$P(B|A)$: the likelihood
$P(A|B)$: the posterior
$P(A|B) = P(A)$: A and B are Independent.
$P(A \cap B|C)=P(A|C)P(B|C)$: A and B are conditionally independent on the occurence of the event C.
